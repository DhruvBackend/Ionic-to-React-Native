4. **loadMap() runs twice on entry** (constructor and ionViewDidLoad), and on every tap of the my_location box and of both Add location buttons (add_loc calls it first). Fix rejected (permission denied, timeout): toast `Error getting location` (3000, bottom). Success: store `new_lat` / `new_long`, update the url, call `getGeoencoder()` (reverseGeocode of hard-coded Berlin coordinates 52.5072095, 13.1452818 and forwardGeocode('Berlin'), results only console.logged; the geocoder plugin is not in the Topbath build) so `geoAddress` stays `''`.
