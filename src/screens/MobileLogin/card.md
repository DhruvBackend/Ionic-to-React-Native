(a) `(keypress)` -> `dbService.onMobileValidateHandler` (dbservice.ts:177-186) blocks any typed char that is not 0-9 (keyCode 8 excepted); paste is NOT filtered. (b) `maxlength=10 minlength=10 required min=0` (`min` does nothing on tel). (c) Continue is `[disabled]` when `!data.mobile_no || data.mobile_no.length != '10'` (loose compare). Disabled buttons get the global `button[disabled]{opacity:.4;pointer-events:none}`, so the label is white at 40% on the green bar. (d) the click runs `f.valid && onLoginSubmitHandler()`. There is no first-digit check and no validation message.
